## About
Nonna's Table is a family-run Italian kitchen. Scratch pasta, wood-fired pizza,
and a short list of classics done well. Takeout and delivery, dine-in on weekends.

## Location & contact
- Address: 118 Saginaw St, Flint, MI 48502
- Phone: (810) 555-0133
- We take orders directly here in chat, or by phone during open hours.

## Hours
- Mon: Closed
- Tue–Thu: 4:00pm – 9:00pm
- Fri–Sat: 12:00pm – 10:00pm
- Sun: 12:00pm – 8:00pm
- Kitchen stops taking new orders 30 minutes before close.

## Fulfillment
- Pickup: ready in about 20–30 minutes at busy times, ~15 minutes when quiet.
- Delivery: within 4 miles of the restaurant. Flat $4 delivery fee. Typical 35–50 minutes.
- We do NOT deliver outside the 4-mile radius. Offer pickup instead.
- Minimum delivery order: $20.
- Payment is taken by the restaurant when they confirm the order (card or cash).

## Dietary & allergens
- Vegetarian and vegan items are marked above.
- Gluten-free pasta available (+$2). Pizza crust is NOT gluten-free.
- Common allergens (dairy, gluten, shellfish, nuts) are present in the kitchen — we
  cannot guarantee zero cross-contact. For a serious allergy, tell the restaurant
  directly when they confirm.
- We do not publish full ingredient/calorie breakdowns; the kitchen can answer
  specific ingredient questions when they confirm the order.
